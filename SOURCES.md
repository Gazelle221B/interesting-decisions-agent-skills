# Sources and attribution

These are original workflows inspired by the sources below. The decision table, failure trace, test protocol and sample game are not Meier's published rules. There are no scans, transcripts, ebook files, or diagrams from the sources in this repository.

| Source | What informed the skills |
| --- | --- |
| Sid Meier, [Interesting Decisions (GDC 2012)](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1015756/Interesting) | Treat choices as one lens on gameplay. Inspect the timing of decisions, the information available before them and the feedback afterward. |
| Sid Meier, [The Psychology of Game Design (GDC 2010)](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1012186/The-Psychology-of-Game) | What players infer from a result can differ from the actual model. Design feedback and agency with that gap in mind. |
| Sid Meier with Jennifer Lee Noonan, [Sid Meier's Memoir! (W. W. Norton, 2020)](https://wwnorton.co.uk/books/9781324005872-sid-meier-s-memoir); [publisher-authorized excerpt](https://www.pcgamesn.com/sid-meiers-memoir-civilization) | The excerpt recalls testing builds with Bruce Shelley, making a large reversible size change to see whether it mattered, and dropping ideas that did not work for Civilization. That supports informative iterations, not an instruction to double every parameter or ban loss mechanics. |
| Contemporary Japanese coverage: [4Gamer (2012)](https://www.4gamer.net/games/105/G010598/20120308054/), [GAME Watch (2010)](https://game.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/354590.html) | Context for the talks and their examples; treat interview and keynote heuristics as design hypotheses, not empirical laws. |

The sample states and criteria are illustrative, not results from a study or playtest. The memoir was consulted selectively, not treated as a complete source survey. For further reading, see the introduction and chapters 10, 11, 16 and 18. Chapter 16 informs why an even pick rate alone is not proof of a good choice. Chapter 11 informs the check for pacing interruptions that pull attention away from the core loop. Chapter 18 informs the check that simplifying a rule has not made the answer automatic.
